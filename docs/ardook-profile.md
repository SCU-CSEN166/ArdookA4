## Mission and the problem it solves

> "To empower mothers of disabled children through sustainable employment while providing exceptional laundry services." [1]

Mothers of children with disabilities in Armenia often can't hold jobs because they must stay home to care for their children. **34%** of them face a very low quality of life. Most families rely on small pensions, and the social benefits they receive (**about €55/month**) are **68% below the minimum wage (about €170)**. [2] Ardook lets these mothers earn "a stable income with dignity, without leaving their children's side." [1]

## How it works

- Customers (individuals, hotels, restaurants, preschools, beauty salons, Airbnb hosts) order by phone, WhatsApp, Facebook or Instagram. [1][3]
- Couriers pick up the items, deliver them to a trained mother who washes and irons them at home, and return them to the customer. Standard turnaround is 24 hours. [1][2][3]
- Ardook provides the equipment, training and ongoing support. Each customer is assigned to a specific mother. [1][3]
- Mothers are paid per order and receive **50% of profits** as bonuses (2023). [3]
- Eco practices: recycled plastic hangers, reusable woven bags instead of plastic, sustainable detergents, optimized water and energy use. [1][2]

## Impact and traction

| When | Mothers | Other figures | Source |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Feb 2023 | 5 working + 5 in training | 160+ customers. Mothers earn about $200/month vs. a minimum wage of about $170. | [3] |
| 2026 | "dozens of mothers"; 50+ families | 5,000+ orders; 4.9 customer rating | [1] |

Ardook tracks its impact with these indicators [2]:
- **Social:** number of mothers employed, income vs. minimum wage, quality of life, and the move from government support to paying taxes.
- **Economic:** total earnings distributed, revenue growth and profitability.
- **Environmental:** water and electricity use, and packaging waste.

Monitoring uses monthly financial reports, surveys, data analytics and external evaluations. Planned next: a digital system to track mothers' income and employment in real time, and partnerships with academic institutions for independent evaluation. [2]
